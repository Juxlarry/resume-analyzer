How Hirivo analyses your resume

Keyword Match Analysis — Hirivo extracts key skills, qualifications, and role-specific competencies from the job description and compares them against your resume, including synonyms and industry-standard variants. Missing keywords are listed by priority.

Format and Structure Scoring — Most ATS platforms reject resumes containing tables, text boxes, graphics, and non-standard section headings. Hirivo identifies every formatting element that may cause a parsing failure.

Job Title Alignment — Your most recent role is compared against the target position. A significant title gap often reduces ATS ranking even when skills match.

Achievement Quantification — Resumes with measurable results rank higher in both ATS and human review. Hirivo flags bullet points that lack metrics.

Rewrite Logic — Hirivo uses OpenAI GPT-4o to rewrite your resume — incorporating missing keywords naturally, restructuring bullets for impact, and removing ATS-hostile formatting. Your experience is never fabricated or misrepresented.
