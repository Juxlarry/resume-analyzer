Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

The short version: Hirivo processes your resume in real time and discards it immediately after analysis. We never store, share, or sell your documents or personal data. Your privacy is protected by design.

1. What Data We Collect

  • Resume file — your uploaded PDF or DOCX, submitted for analysis. Processed in real time and immediately discarded. Never saved to disk or database.
  • Job description text — pasted text or uploaded file. Processed in real time and immediately discarded.
  • Account information — if you register: your email address and encrypted password. We do not store payment card details.
  • Anonymous usage analytics — page views and feature interactions used to improve the product. No personal identifiers attached.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your resume and job description are transmitted to OpenAI's API for analysis and rewriting. This processing is real-time and session-only. Hirivo does not retain any document content after your results are delivered.

Your email address is used to manage your account, send transactional emails (password reset, login activity), and — only with your explicit consent — product updates. We never send unsolicited commercial email.

3. Data Retention

✓ Zero document retention. Hirivo does not store resumes, CVs, cover letters, or job descriptions.

Resume and job description files exist only in memory during processing and are discarded immediately after results are returned to you. Account data (email address) is retained while your account is active and permanently deleted within 30 days of an account deletion request to privacy@hirivo.com.

5. Your Rights

Under the Ghana Data Protection Act 2012 (Act 843), Nigeria's NDPR, the EU/UK GDPR, and California's CCPA, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your account data
  • Object to or restrict processing of your data
  • Withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent

As Hirivo stores no resume or document data, the right to erasure for document content is satisfied by design. To exercise your rights for account data, contact privacy@hirivo.com.
